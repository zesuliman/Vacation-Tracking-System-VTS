**### Project Description ###** 
A dynamic Vacation Tracking System designed for multi-manager organizational structures. This system enables employees to 
manage their leave and time-off requests while providing managers with a centralized view to track and schedule leaves across multiple projects and cross-functional teams.

**### Vision ###** 
A Vacation Tracking System (VTS) will provide individual employees with the
capability to manage their own vacation time, sick leave, and personal time off,
without having to be an expert in company policy or the local facility’s leave
policies.
